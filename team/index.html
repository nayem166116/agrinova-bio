Team

The people who sign off the batch and walk the field.

AgriNova Bio is 38 people: 11 field agronomists, 9 in fermentation and quality control, 6 in RNA and strain research, and the rest in logistics, regulatory and administration. Everyone who advises on a programme has walked a field in the territory they advise on.

Leadership

Four people you will actually meet

There is no account manager layer. The agronomist who assesses your field is the one who writes your season review.

01

Managing Director

Twenty-two years in arable agronomy across the Netherlands, Germany and northern France. Joined from a cooperative advisory role in 2016 to build a company that would publish the trials where the product did nothing.

Wageningen · Agronomy, strategy, territory registration

02

Head of Research

PhD in soil microbiology, previously eight years on rhizosphere colonisation in a public research institute. Owns strain selection, the qPCR colonisation thresholds and the decision on which candidate strains never reach a label.

Wageningen · Strain research, colonisation assays, trial design

03

Head of Production and Quality

Runs the 4,200 m² Lelystad fermentation and formulation site. Signs the release CFU count on every batch and the end-of-life specification that the batch certificate is measured against.

Lelystad · Fermentation, formulation, batch release

04

Head of Field Agronomy

Leads the 11 field agronomists and the strip-trial network across nine countries. Responsible for the untreated strip rule and for the assessments that end in a recommendation not to buy.

Field-based · Trials, programme assessment, season reviews
