Questions we get asked before signing

Plain answers

Q

Is there a yield guarantee?

No. 23% of trial sites showed no measurable response and we will not price a guarantee that the data does not support. What is guaranteed is the CFU count in the pack and the measurement work around it.

Q

Can I buy the products without a programme?

For repeat customers, yes. For a first season, no — without the assessment and the untreated strip neither of us learns anything.

Q

What happens if the season is a write-off?

Weather losses are not covered. Product that fails its release specification is replaced or credited in full, verified against the batch certificate.

Q

Is this organic-certified?

RhizoSet, TerraCore and ResiduePrime are listed for organic production in the Netherlands, Germany and France. StomaGuard is not listed in all territories — check the register for your country before ordering.
