Programme

What a programme actually contains.

A programme is four decisions, three measurements and one untreated strip. The products are the smallest part of it.

01

Field assessment

A soil test, a penetrometer transect and last season's yield map. This is where roughly one field in five is told that drainage, lime or compaction work will beat any biological input available.

Deliverable: written field assessment

02

Programme design

Product selection, rates, application windows and the compatibility check against the existing spray plan. Each product has to justify its place or it is removed from the quote.

Deliverable: programme sheet with rates and windows

03

Application support

Tank order, water quality, nozzle and timing guidance, plus a call before the first application. Most disappointing results trace back to this step, not to the biology.

Deliverable: application card per product

04

Measurement

Stand counts, tissue tests, harvest data from the treated and untreated strips, and a written season review that states plainly whether the programme paid.

Deliverable: season review with strip comparison
