Label specifications

Four products, four jobs, no overlap.

Every specification below is the label specification. Concentrations are release values verified per batch; rates are the registered range, not a marketing range. If a field does not meet the stated condition, the product will underperform and we would rather say so here.

01 · RS-04

RhizoSet SC 04

Seed and in-furrow suspension concentrate

RhizoSet is the entry point of most programmes because it works in the two weeks that decide the season. Two Bacillus strains and one fluorescent Pseudomonas colonise the seminal root surface within 72 hours of imbibition, competing with early damping-off pressure and releasing siderophores that keep iron available in high-pH soils. It is a colonisation product, not a nutrient product: it does not replace a starter fertiliser and will not compensate for a poor seedbed.

02 · TC-12

TerraCore WG 12

Water-dispersible granule for soil establishment

TerraCore addresses phosphorus that is present in the soil but not available to the crop. It pairs arbuscular mycorrhizal propagules with two phosphate-solubilising bacterial strains that lower the pH of the immediate root micro-environment. The response is largest on fields with an Olsen-P between 12 and 25 mg/kg and a history of intensive tillage; on high-P soils with recent manure applications, we tell growers to expect little and often advise against the spend.

03 · SG-07

StomaGuard EC 07

Foliar emulsifiable concentrate for stress windows

StomaGuard is applied before a forecast stress event, not after it. Seaweed-derived osmolytes and free amino acids support turgor maintenance and reduce the duration of stomatal closure during heat and short-term water deficit. Applied after visible wilting, the measured effect is close to zero — which is why the label, the agronomy team and the readiness tool all push the same 3-to-7-day lead time. It is compatible with most foliar programmes but must go into the tank last.

04 · RP-03

ResiduePrime WP 03

Post-harvest wettable powder for residue breakdown

ResiduePrime is the least glamorous product in the line and the one that changes the following season most reliably. Two Trichoderma species and a cellulolytic Bacillus accelerate the breakdown of cereal and maize residue, returning nitrogen to the following crop earlier and cutting the green bridge for stubble-borne disease. It needs moisture: on dry standing stubble with no rain in the forecast, the application should be delayed rather than wasted.

Where we start

The product is the last decision, not the first.

Before a programme is quoted, four things are checked. If any of them fails, a biological input is the wrong purchase that season and our agronomists will say so in writing.

01

Drainage and compaction come first

A penetrometer reading above 2.5 MPa in the top 30 cm caps the response of every product in the line. Root colonisation cannot happen in a pan. Fix the structure, then inoculate.

02

Soil chemistry has to be in range

Below pH 5.2 the Bacillus strains in RhizoSet survive poorly. Lime first; the response the following season is consistently larger than anything the inoculant delivers on acid ground.

03

The tank-mix has to be survivable

Several seed-applied fungicides reduce viable counts by more than 60% within four hours of contact. We publish a compatibility list and will refuse an order that cannot be applied safely.

04

Someone has to be able to measure it

Every programme includes at least one untreated strip. Without it there is no way to separate the product from the season, and a programme that cannot be measured will not be renewed.

Root colonisation, in sequence

Structure, chemistry, tank-mix and measurement are checked in that order. Each one that fails caps everything below it.

How a season runs

Four contact points between drilling and stubble.

01

Establishment

RhizoSet SC 04 goes on with the seed. Colonisation of the seminal roots is measurable by qPCR within 72 hours, and the stand count at 14 days is the first checkpoint of the programme.

Window: drilling · Checkpoint: 14-day stand count

02

Root architecture

TerraCore WG 12 is applied from planting to V6, where the mycorrhizal propagules still have young root tissue to colonise. Phosphorus uptake is confirmed by tissue test at V8.

Window: planting to V6 · Checkpoint: V8 tissue test

03

Stress window

StomaGuard EC 07 is timed off the 10-day forecast rather than the calendar. Applied 3 to 7 days ahead of a heat or moisture event, it shortens the period of stomatal closure.

Window: pre-stress · Checkpoint: canopy temperature

04

Residue and reset

ResiduePrime WP 03 goes onto moist stubble within three weeks of harvest, returning nitrogen earlier to the following crop and cutting the green bridge for stubble-borne disease.

Window: within 21 days of harvest · Checkpoint: spring residue score

What the trials show

Including the sites where nothing happened.

01

The mean is not the story

Across 412 replicated strips the mean cereal response was +4.1%. The distribution matters more: 23% of sites showed no measurable difference, and 6% came in slightly below the untreated strip.

Source: AgriNova field programme 2019–2025

02

Soil type predicts most of it

Sandy loams with organic matter under 2.5% produced roughly double the mean response of heavy clays. We publish the split rather than the average, because the average sells better than it advises.

Split by texture class, n=412

03

Third season, different question

Fields on the full programme for three seasons show aggregate stability and earlier nitrogen release from residue. Those effects are slower and harder to sell than a yield number, and they are the ones that persist.

Long-term sites, n=38
