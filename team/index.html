Team

Thirty-eight people, eleven of them in the field.

Colonize Bio was founded in 2022 by two soil microbiologists and an arable agronomist. The company is deliberately weighted towards field agronomy and quality control rather than sales, because the assessment and the batch specification are what the product depends on.

Leadership

Who is accountable for what

There is no account management layer. The agronomist who assesses a field is the one who writes the season review for it.

01

Joris van Alphen — Managing Director

Twenty-two years in row-crop agronomy across Minnesota, Iowa and the eastern Dakotas, latterly in a cooperative advisory role. Co-founded Colonize Bio in 2022 to build a company that would publish the trials in which the product did nothing.

Research and collaboration

Where the work is done with others.

Strip trials are run with growers and independent advisers rather than in-house only, because a result we produce and score ourselves is not evidence.

·

Grower trial network

412 replicated strip trials across nine states since 2023, hosted on commercial fields under normal farm operations.

·

Independent scoring

Stand counts, tissue tests and residue scoring on long-term sites are read by advisers who are not paid on product volume.

·

Published distribution

Every trial entered into the programme database appears in the published response distribution, including the sites that came in below the untreated strip.

Research, agronomy and press enquiries reach the team at agronomy@colonize.bio, or through the contact page.
