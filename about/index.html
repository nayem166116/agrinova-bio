Company

A small agronomy company that happens to ferment things.

AgriNova Bio was founded in 2016 in Wageningen by two soil microbiologists and a former arable agronomist who had spent a decade watching biological products sold without measurement. The trial archive they started keeping is now the training set behind the AI platform.

01

How it started

The first two seasons were contract trial work for other manufacturers. That work produced a strain library and a strong opinion: most products failed because of application context, not because of the organism.

2016–2018 · Wageningen

02

Manufacturing

Fermentation and formulation moved in-house in 2019 at a facility in Lelystad. Every batch is released against a CFU specification, and the certificate travels with the pallet.

2019 · Lelystad, 4,200 m²

03

Today

38 people, of whom 11 are field agronomists. Nine countries in the register. We have turned down distribution offers in three more because we could not staff the assessment work behind them.

2026 · 38 staff

What we will not do

Three commitments that cost us revenue

01

No yield guarantees

The data does not support them and a guarantee priced into the product is just a more expensive product.

02

No selective trial reporting

Every trial entered into the programme database appears in the published distribution, including the 6% of sites that came in below the untreated strip.

03

No sale without an assessment

First-season customers get a field assessment before a quote. Roughly one in five is advised to spend the money on structure instead.

Models find the pattern. Biology only works when the agronomy around it is right.
