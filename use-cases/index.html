Field use

Four situations, four honest answers.

These are the field patterns where the programme is most often specified, written as they appear in the season review rather than as case-study prose.

01

Winter wheat on light land, Renville County, Minnesota

840 acres, sandy loam, organic matter 1.9%. RhizoSet on all seed, TerraCore on the two lowest-P blocks. Treated strips averaged +6.2% over three seasons; the block with the highest P index showed no difference and TerraCore was dropped there.

Programme: establishment and root · 3 seasons

02

Corn under irrigation, Kearney County, Nebraska

470 acres with a reliable July heat window. StomaGuard timed off the NOAA forecast, two applications. Canopy temperature ran 2.5 °F lower on treated strips during the event; grain moisture at harvest was unchanged.

Programme: stress window · 2 seasons

03

Canola, Grand Forks County, North Dakota

640 acres with heavy stubble and a tight rotation. ResiduePrime after wheat, RhizoSet at planting. Spring residue scores improved by two points on the assessment scale and establishment was more even; yield difference was inside noise.

Programme: residue and establishment · 2 seasons

04

Where we said no

A 220-acre block in Story County, Iowa with a compaction pan at 9 inches and pH 5.0. The assessment recommended subsoiling and lime instead of a programme. The grower returned two seasons later and the establishment response was then clearly positive.

Outcome: no sale in season one

Models find the pattern. Biology only works when the agronomy around it is right.
